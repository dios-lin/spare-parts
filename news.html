01 // TRANSMISSION & CLUTCHISO 9001 VERIFIED

How to select OEM replacement clutch assemblies?

Proper OEM clutch specification requires pairing friction surface thermal dissipation with peak crankshaft torque and operational RPM limits.

Friction Plate Material

Sintered bronze-carbon composite friction plates are calibrated for aggressive high-RPM engagement. Organic cellulose matrix linings are reserved for standard commuter setups below 125cc.

Spring Tension Overhead

Diaphragm and coil spring packs must provide +25% torque overhead above peak crank output to eliminate downshift clutch flare on wet and dry multi-plate layouts.
