## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of elite motorsport competition and aerospace-grade industrial manufacturing. Tailored for enterprise tier-1 procurement managers, performance race teams, and global motorcycle OEMs, the visual language establishes authority through unyielding precision, measured aggression, and technical mastery.

The interface evokes the visceral tactility of a 5-axis CNC-machined titanium triple clamp combined with the clinical precision of telemetry consoles found in MotoGP pit lanes. It uses a high-contrast dark aesthetic that combines dark glassmorphism, crisp hairline metallic borders, and vivid racing telemetry indicators. Visual clutter is eliminated in favor of information density, micro-calibrated spacing, and absolute structural clarity.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around low-reflectance metallic substrates and laser-focused optical accents:

- **Surface & Foundation (Neutrals):**
  - Void Black (`#0A0A0C`): Baseline application canvas, preventing visual fatigue in mission-critical environments.
  - Graphite Core (`#111116`): Layer 1 containers, structural panels, and drawer surfaces.
  - Billet Slate (`#181820`): Card surfaces, elevated tables, and input wells.
  - Anodized Charcoal (`#262632`): Hairline dividers, subtle framing borders, and inactive states.

- **Metallic Accents (Secondary Palette):**
  - Titanium Polish (`#E2E8F0`): Primary high-contrast technical headings, active values, and hero iconography.
  - Machined Steel (`#94A3B8`): Secondary telemetry labels, standard body copy, and peripheral units.
  - Cast Iron (`#64748B`): Inactive iconography, placeholder text, and structural grid lines.

- **Kinetic Accents (Primary & Tertiary Palette):**
  - Racing Velocity Crimson (`#FF2E00`): Primary interactive triggers, active state indicators, critical limit telemetry, and high-priority RFQ actions.
  - Circuit Red (`#E11D48`): System alerts, destructive states, and secondary thermal warnings.
  - Tachometer Amber (`#F59E0B`): Calibration states, intermediate stock alerts, and engineering deviations.
  - Precision Cyan (`#06B6D4`): Digital twin active links, CAD export actions, and tolerance pass verifications.

## Typography

Typography establishes an engineered balance between aerodynamic momentum and numerical precision:

- **Headings (Space Grotesk):** Delivers a technical, sharp-contoured architectural cadence. Uppercase application is strictly reserved for `label-caps` and primary KPI identifiers. All primary headings leverage tight letter-spacing to simulate aerodynamic tension.
- **Body & Continuous Copy (Geist):** Clean, neutral, high x-height grotesque rendering guarantees instantaneous legibility across dense spec sheets, engineering manuals, and cross-border customs manifests.
- **Data & Telemetry (JetBrains Mono):** Applied across all CAD references, dimensional tolerances (e.g., `±0.005mm`), batch serializations, torque specifications, and metallurgical grade numbers. Numbers must render in tabular lining figures (`tnum`) to maintain vertical scanning fidelity in spec matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an architectural 12-column rigid grid system optimized for dense multi-axis data display, exploded CAD visualizers, and supply-chain logistics matrices:

- **Rhythm & Increments:** Pure 4px base increment system (`0.25rem`). Component heights follow standardized industrial intervals: 32px (dense spec row), 40px (standard action), 48px (primary operation), and 56px (command header).
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px margins, fluid stacking. Complex specification matrices horizontally scroll within pinned-left reference columns.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, 32px margins, 20px gutters. Collapsible side-rails for CAD model inspection.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px to 1680px max):** 12 columns, 64px margins, 24px gutters. Tri-pane layouts support concurrent viewports: component selection hierarchy, interactive 3D digital-twin inspection, and real-time ERP pricing/lead-time recalculation.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons diffuse organic drop shadows, replacing them with sharp specular metallic highlights and frosted obsidian substrates:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Solid Void Black (`#0A0A0C`).
  - **Tier 1 (Panels & Frames):** Graphite Core (`#111116`) backed by `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.08)`.
  - **Tier 2 (Interactive Modules):** Dark glassmorphic substrate with `rgba(24, 24, 32, 0.75)`, combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an inner top-edge specular highlight `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
  - **Tier 3 (Modals & Flyouts):** Solid Billet Slate (`#181820`) elevated via dual-perimeter framing: an inner `1px solid rgba(255, 46, 0, 0.35)` and an ambient, laser-tinted shadow `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 20px -5px rgba(255, 46, 0, 0.15)`.

- **Active Telemetry Glows:** Real-time state indicators utilize focused 8px Gaussian blurs tinted with Racing Velocity Crimson (`rgba(255, 46, 0, 0.45)`) or Precision Cyan (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.45)`), establishing an optical aesthetic reminiscent of an active dyno instrumentation bench.

## Shapes

The design system strictly employs a sharp geometry (`roundedness: 0`). Curved radii are eliminated to align with high-precision CNC end-mill cut paths, cold-rolled billet alloy, and automotive carbon-composite chassis plates:

- **Corner Architecture:** Absolutely square 0px borders across standard inputs, tables, status pills, and modal assemblies.
- **Chamfer Engineering:** High-impact callouts, hero CTA buttons, and certification badges may incorporate a 45-degree corner cut (6px diagonal chamfer) on top-right and bottom-left vertices, achieved via CSS `clip-path: polygon(...)`. This visual metaphor directly references structural weight-relief machining in competition swingarms and triple clamps.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary Kinetic:* Sharp 0px corners, Velocity Crimson background (`#FF2E00`), contrasting dark text (`#0A0A0C`), Space Grotesk Bold uppercase font. Hover activates a high-intensity white border flash with an outer red laser glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(255, 46, 0, 0.6)`).
  - *Machined Secondary:* Dark transparent glass canvas (`rgba(24, 24, 32, 0.8)`), 1px brushed steel border (`#94A3B8`), Titanium text (`#E2E8F0`). Hover shifts border to white with a 5% surface luminance lift.
  - *Telemetry Ghost:* Borderless, JetBrains Mono font, paired with a right-aligned precision crosshair icon (`+`) that rotates 45 degrees on hover.

- **Badges & Engineering Certifications:**
  - Monospaced, high-contrast micro-tags (e.g., `CNC 5-AXIS`, `ISO/TS 16949`, `DIN 931-TI`).
  - Solid graphite substrate with 1px hairline silver border, accompanied by a 6px status LED dot on the left. Pass states illuminate cyan (`#06B6D4`), live telemetry pulses red (`#FF2E00`).

- **Cards & Data Modules:**
  - Encased in frosted obsidian with brushed titanium hairline edges.
  - Header segments include an integrated engineering coordinate rule (e.g., `SEC_04 // TOLERANCE_MATRIX`).
  - Chamfered accent notches on top-right edges signal deep CAD inspection functionality.

- **Inputs & Form Controls:**
  - *Text Fields:* Flush bottom-line priority or full 1px anodized frame. Void background, JetBrains Mono value inputs, and Space Grotesk floating micro-labels. Focus states trigger a crisp `#FF2E00` border without layout shift.
  - *Checkboxes & Selectors:* Strict square architecture (0px radius). Checked status displays a solid racing-crimson inner block with no rounded bevels.

- **Tables & Tolerance Matrices:**
  - Dense tabular data layouts featuring zebra rows of `#0A0A0C` alternating with `#111116`.
  - Column dividers rendered via hairline 1px `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.05)`. Numerical columns align strictly right with JetBrains Mono numbers.

- **Telemetry & Metric Strips:**
  - High-impact KPI blocks showing active production run speed, metallurgical density checks, and global stock allocations.
  - Metric values render in oversized `code-metric` typography, anchored by miniature sparkline charts rendered with single-pixel non-smoothed polyline vectors.
