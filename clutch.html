RACE-SPEC SLIPPER CLUTCH & BACK-TORQUE LIMITER ASSEMBLY

LINCCORE Slipper Clutches feature a patented ramp-and-ball mechanical back-torque limiter system designed to eliminate rear wheel hop during aggressive corner-entry downshifts. Precision machined from aerospace 7075-T6 billet aluminum with hard-anodized basket surfaces and Kevlar/friction composite plate packs.
